About the Society

Founded in 2021, the Medical Research Society is a student-led organization where high schoolers design studies, review each other's work, and publish real research.

We started with a simple observation: students are full of questions about health and medicine, but there was nowhere at our school to pursue those questions rigorously. The Medical Research Society exists to close that gap. Each year, our members propose research questions, form teams, design studies under faculty supervision, and submit their findings to our student editorial board for peer review.

Our work spans the full landscape of medicine — from survey-based public health studies conducted in our own hallways, to literature reviews of cutting-edge gene therapy trials, to computational projects applying machine learning to clinical data. What unites the portfolio is method: every paper that appears in our journal has survived at least two rounds of peer review and an ethics check.

Membership is open to all students, regardless of prior experience. New members begin with our Research Foundations track — journal club, study-design workshops, and a mentored first project — before leading their own studies.

Our Commitment to Ethics

Research involving people carries responsibilities. Before any data collection begins, every study proposal must pass our internal ethics review, which checks for informed consent, anonymity, minimal risk, and voluntary participation — modeled on the principles of an institutional review board. Surveys are anonymous by default, participation is always optional, and any study involving sensitive topics is reviewed jointly by student editors and our faculty advisor.

We also hold ourselves to standards of honesty in reporting. Negative results are published alongside positive ones, limitations are stated plainly, and every author signs a statement confirming the data is their own.

Questions about our process? Email the editorial board at journal@ourschool.edu.

Submit a Manuscript

Any member may submit a manuscript during our open submission windows (mid-September and late February). Submissions go through two rounds of peer review by the editorial board, typically taking three to five weeks.

What we accept: original research, survey studies, systematic and narrative literature reviews, and case-based analyses. Papers should be 1,500–4,000 words with an abstract of 150–250 words, formatted with sections for Background, Methods, Results, and Discussion where applicable.

Before you submit:

1. Confirm your study passed our internal ethics review if it involved human participants.
2. Include a signed statement that the work is original and the data are your own.
3. Cite sources in AMA or APA style — our editors can help with formatting.
4. Email your manuscript as a PDF, Word document, or Google Doc link to journal@ourschool.edu.

Rejected manuscripts receive detailed reviewer comments and an invitation to revise and resubmit. We publish negative results as readily as positive ones.
